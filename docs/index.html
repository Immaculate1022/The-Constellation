Black holes and stars are not opposites. The singularity is not a drain; it is a valve and a foundry. Nothing here violates thermodynamics — entropy is locally managed by topological routing, never eliminated. The IOF is the same loop, engineered in thin-film lithium niobate.

TFLN hardware test plan — Rev 0.2 ablation matrix

Four devices on one die, changing one variable at a time. Select a device to see what it isolates and what would count as a result. Rev 0.2 corrections applied: racetrack FSR ≈ 0.84 nm (104.6 GHz), and the delay spiral lengthened to ≈27.3 cm so τ ≈ 2 ns is real, with its area and loss booked into the loop budget. The thermal channel is mandatory on every device, and the power budget must close — that is what keeps the study Second-Law honest.

Testable predictions

Q-factor correlation. Black hole systems with higher mass-to-radiation ratios should exhibit resonance quality metrics analogous to Q-factors — measurable via gravitational-wave ringdown signatures.

Phase-locked photon paths. Gravitational lensing should show systematic phase relationships between deflected photon paths — not random, but topologically constrained.

Stated as predictions, not conclusions: both are checkable against data that exists or is coming, and both can fail. That is the point of writing them down.

Möbius-Llama — the reflection hook Demonstration diagram

A wrapper for any LLM: the generation stream passes through a hook, the model walks a Möbius loop through its own reasoning — one half-twist, so it comes back facing the other side of its own thought — and only then continues. Weight-preserving: nothing is retrained, nothing is overwritten. Reflection is a route, not a rewrite.

Emergency state recovery — confidence-weighted rollback Demonstration diagram

The same pattern as the topographic reasoner, wearing a different uniform: keep scored snapshots of known good states, and when the present goes wrong, return to the best one — weighted by how much you trust it and how long ago it was true. Memory as a safety system, not a diary.

IOF Design Grammar — principles

Resonance over force. Tune a system until it wants to do the thing; don't push it there every cycle. What is forced dissipates — what resonates persists.

Loops close. Every output is somebody's input, including the waste stream. A design that throws energy or information away has a hole in it, and the hole is where the future leaks out.

Topology is behavior. The shape of the connections decides what the system can do before any component is chosen. Get the manifold right and the physics follows.

Memory has geometry. Store states with their landscape — what was near them, what they were climbing toward — or don't be surprised when recall brings back the wrong mountain.

Phase is information. An answer can precipitate from a wave pattern instead of being assembled bit by bit. Not every question wants a sequence.

Open by default. The fabric is published — source, licenses, failures included — because coexistence can't be built on systems nobody is allowed to inspect.
